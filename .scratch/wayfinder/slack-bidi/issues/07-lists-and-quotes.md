# Lists and quotes

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03

## Question

Where do list bullets/numbers and the quote bar go in mixed content? (a) per item, following the item's direction (Docs), (b) per list, following the first item.

Recommendation: (a).

## Answer

(a) Per item: each list item and quote line gets its own direction and alignment; bullet/number and quote bar follow the item's side. Consistent with the Paragraph unit decision (P1).
