# Privacy Policy - Slack Hebrew RTL Lite

Effective: September 28, 2026

Slack Hebrew RTL Lite reads text on Slack pages in your browser to determine how to display right-to-left and left-to-right lines. It changes the display of messages and adds direction buttons and shortcuts to the composer.

When you press Enter to send, it automatically inserts an invisible Unicode direction mark (U+200E or U+200F) at the start of any line whose first letter points the wrong way for that line. The direction buttons and Ctrl+[ / Ctrl+] also add or remove this mark on the current line. These marks become part of the message you send through Slack.

When you copy selected messages, it removes invisible direction marks at the start of lines from what you copy.

No data leaves your browser because of this extension. It does not send page content to a developer-operated server or use analytics. Nothing is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties. It stores nothing: no messages, settings, or identifiers are saved on your device or elsewhere. Slack's own handling of messages remains governed by Slack.

Changes to this policy are published in this file's commit history.
