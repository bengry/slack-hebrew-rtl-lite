# Privacy Policy - Slack Hebrew RTL Lite

Slack Hebrew RTL Lite reads text on Slack pages in your browser to determine how to display right-to-left and left-to-right lines. It changes the display of messages and adds direction buttons and shortcuts to the composer. To help recipients see your intended direction, it may insert invisible Unicode direction marks into lines you compose; those marks are part of the message you send through Slack. When you copy selected messages, it attempts to remove line-leading direction marks from the copied content. The extension does not send page content to a developer-operated server, use analytics, or maintain its own storage of Slack messages. Slack's own handling of messages remains governed by Slack.

Source code: https://github.com/bengry/slack-hebrew-rtl-lite
